# Design Direction & Visual Taste

- Visual Theme: "Warm Editorial Modern" — clean paper/warm-tinted surfaces, generous negative space, crisp typography, and subtle rounded card borders.
- Notes Cards: Styled with light background tints (Sage, Lavender, Warm Peach/Amber, Sky Blue, Soft Rose, Cream/Neutral) with crisp dark text in light mode, and deep desaturated card tints in dark mode.
- Pin Indicators: Subtle slanted pin icon on pinned notes.
- Touch Targets: >= 48dp on all interactive buttons.
- Search Bar: M3-styled floating or docked search field with rounded corners, clear action, and search icon.
- Bottom Sheet Editor: Clean title field (large font, borderless), multiline body text field, color selection row, and pin/trash actions in the toolbar.
